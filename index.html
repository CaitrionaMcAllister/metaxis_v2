Who we are↗

Who
are we

Metaxis is a directional audio system. Using precise, focused sound steering, we create personalised audio zones inside a single space.

So every visitor hears the right content, in their language, from exactly the right direction.

No headsets. No devices. No compromise.

What we do↗

Conventional speakers are a floodlight. Metaxis is a spotlight.

An invisible audio zone for each visitor. Tap a visitor in the room to hear what reaches them.

  • Multiple independent, personalised sound zones

  • Different languages
  • All at the same time

Overarching goals↗

Goals

↗Goal 01

Redefine the industry standard for inclusive design

Move inclusion from a compliance obligation to a creative and commercial foundation, so designing for everyone becomes the default.

↗Goal 02

Build the economic case for belonging

Show that inclusive spaces drive measurable gains in footfall, revenue, tourism and community engagement. Accessibility and profitability are the same argument.

↗Goal 03

Shape how cities and communities experience culture together

Anyone, from any background, in any city, can walk into a cultural space and feel it was made for them.
